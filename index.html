About Us

Building Love Stories
Since 2012

RD Weddings is a full-service luxury wedding planning and event design studio based in New Delhi. For over a decade, we have been crafting extraordinary celebrations for India's most discerning families — from intimate ceremonies in palace courtyards to grand multi-day weddings at the world's finest venues.

We bring together years of experience across every aspect of wedding planning — from venue curation and decor design to vendor management and ritual coordination — to deliver a seamlessly beautiful experience for you and every one of your guests.
